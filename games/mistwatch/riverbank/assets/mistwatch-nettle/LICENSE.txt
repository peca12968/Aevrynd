Imported 2026-10-11, Asia/Taipei. Official API MD5/size and local SHA256 are
recorded in provenance/prepare-receipt.json and provenance/official-files.json.

Six original LOD0 plants: medium a/b, small a/b, tall a/b. These are distinct
plants, not six LOD levels. Their display-only X translations were removed;
original local pivots, vertices, UVs and triangles were retained. Native AEMESH
conversion reflects Z and reverses triangle winding for Aevrynd's coordinate
system. Native authoring may uniformly scale these plants; that is an explicit
game-content choice and not a claim that the original scan has different units.

Three 2048x2048 DDS textures with 12 mip levels: base BC7 sRGB, normal BC5
linear, packed AO/roughness/metallic BC7 linear. The independent official alpha
mask is used at cutoff 0.5. RGB border dilation does not alter alpha coverage.
OpenGL normal green is inverted once to DirectX. Packed ARM channels are
preserved. Both source and cooked receipts are retained.

recipes/Prepare.py is the exact one-off Root preparation recipe, preserved
for audit. Its original paths point to the preparation environment and it
refuses to overwrite existing artifacts. It is not an installed background
service, automatic downloader, or portable end-user command.

Acquisition and cooking do not prove world placement, animation, native GPU
rendering, performance or visual acceptance. Those require separate evidence.
